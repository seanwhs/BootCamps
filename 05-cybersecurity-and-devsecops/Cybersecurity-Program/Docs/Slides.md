**Slide 1.28: Risk Appetite Statement**

- **Components:**

  - Risk tolerance (how much)
  - Risk boundaries (what we don't accept)

**Slide 1.29: Risk Appetite Example**
- **Visual:** Risk appetite scale
- **We Accept:**
  - Controlled risks for innovation
  - Calculated risks for growth
  - Residual risks within tolerance
- **We Don't Accept:**
  - Business viability threats
  - Regulatory non-compliance
  - Reputational damage
  - Customer trust loss

**Slide 1.30: Risk Tolerance Levels**
- **Visual:** Tolerance table
- **Levels:**
  - Financial Impact: >$5M → Immediate escalation
  - Regulatory: Any violation → Legal notification
  - Reputational: Major exposure → Board notification
  - Operational: >4-hour outage → Crisis activation
  - Data Breach: >100K records → Regulatory reporting

**Slide 1.31: Risk Calculation**
- **Visual:** Calculation formula
- **Formula:** Risk = Likelihood × Impact × Velocity
  - Likelihood: 1-5 (probability)
  - Impact: 1-5 (consequence)
  - Velocity: 1-5 (speed)
  - Risk Score: 1-125

**Slide 1.32: Risk Scoring Matrix**
- **Visual:** Scoring table
- **Likelihood:**
  - 1: Rare (10+ years)
  - 2: Unlikely (5-10 years)
  - 3: Possible (1-5 years)
  - 4: Likely (multiple/year)
  - 5: Almost certain (frequent)

**Slide 1.33: Risk Levels**
- **Visual:** Risk level matrix
- **Levels:**
  - 1-5: Low → Accept/monitor
  - 6-12: Medium → Mitigate
  - 13-19: High → Immediate mitigation
  - 20-25: Critical → Emergency response

**Slide 1.34: Risk Assessment Process**
- **Visual:** Process flowchart
- **Steps:**
  1. Identification (assets, threats, vulnerabilities)
  2. Analysis (impact, likelihood, controls)
  3. Evaluation (compare to appetite, prioritize)
  4. Treatment (accept, mitigate, transfer, avoid)
  5. Monitoring (track, review, report)

**Slide 1.35: Risk Register**
- **Visual:** Register sample
- **Fields:**
  - Risk ID
  - Risk Owner
  - Description
  - Category
  - Likelihood
  - Impact
  - Velocity
  - Risk Score
  - Risk Level
  - Controls
  - Action Plan
  - Timeline
  - Status

**Slide 1.36: Risk Register Sample**
- **Visual:** Completed register
- **Example:**
  - ID: RISK-2024-001
  - Owner: CISO
  - Description: Unauthorized access to PII
  - Category: Data Security
  - Score: 36 (High)
  - Controls: MFA, RBAC
  - Action: Implement DLP

**Slide 1.37: Risk Treatment Options**
- **Visual:** Treatment options
- **Options:**
  1. Accept: Do nothing (within appetite)
  2. Mitigate: Implement controls
  3. Transfer: Insurance, vendor agreements
  4. Avoid: Stop the activity

**Slide 1.38: Risk Treatment Decision Tree**
- **Visual:** Decision tree graphic
- **Process:**
  - Is risk within appetite?
    - Yes → Accept
    - No → Can we mitigate?
      - Yes → Implement controls
      - No → Can we transfer?
        - Yes → Transfer
        - No → Avoid

**Slide 1.39: Risk Monitoring and Reporting**
- **Visual:** Reporting cadence
- **Frequency:**
  - Risk Register: Weekly
  - Risk Dashboard: Monthly
  - Risk Summary: Quarterly
  - Annual Report: Annually

**Slide 1.40: Key Risk Indicators (KRIs)**
- **Visual:** KRI table
- **Indicators:**
  - Open critical risks > 10 → CISO escalation
  - Remediation time > 90 days → Executive Council
  - Third-party exceptions > 5% → Procurement
  - Regulatory findings open → CLO
  - Incident frequency > 2/month → Executive Council
