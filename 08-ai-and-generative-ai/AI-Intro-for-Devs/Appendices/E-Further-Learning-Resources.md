## Books

### Foundational AI & ML

| Title | Author(s) | Why Read |
|-------|-----------|----------|
| **"Deep Learning"** | Ian Goodfellow, Yoshua Bengio, Aaron Courville | The definitive textbook on deep learning |
| **"Pattern Recognition and Machine Learning"** | Christopher Bishop | Classic ML textbook with clear explanations |
| **"The Elements of Statistical Learning"** | Trevor Hastie, Robert Tibshirani, Jerome Friedman | Comprehensive statistical learning reference |
| **"Machine Learning: A Probabilistic Perspective"** | Kevin P. Murphy | Broad coverage of ML with probabilistic approach |

### NLP & LLMs

| Title | Author(s) | Why Read |
|-------|-----------|----------|
| **"Speech and Language Processing"** | Daniel Jurafsky, James H. Martin | Comprehensive NLP textbook (free online) |
| **"Natural Language Processing with Transformers"** | Lewis Tunstall, Leandro von Werra, Thomas Wolf | Practical guide to transformers and LLMs |
| **"The Transformer Architecture"** | Various | Papers and tutorials on transformer models |
| **"Prompt Engineering Guide"** | DAIR.AI | Comprehensive guide to prompt engineering |

### AI Engineering & Production

| Title | Author(s) | Why Read |
|-------|-----------|----------|

| **"Designing Machine Learning Systems"** | Chip Huyen | Production ML systems and architecture |
| **"Machine Learning Engineering"** | Andriy Burkov | ML engineering best practices |
| **"The Pragmatic Programmer"** | David Thomas, Andrew Hunt | Timeless software engineering wisdom |

### Agentic AI & RAG

3. **Blogs**: Hugging Face Blog, Towards Data Science
4. **Community**: r/MachineLearning, Hugging Face Discord

### Intermediate Path
1. **Books**: "Designing Machine Learning Systems"
2. **Courses**: DeepLearning.AI LLM courses
3. **Blogs**: LangChain Blog, Pinecone Blog
4. **Community**: LangChain Discord, r/LocalLLaMA

### Advanced Path
1. **Papers**: "Attention Is All You Need", ReAct, Self-RAG
2. **Courses**: Advanced RAG, Multi-Agent Systems
3. **Research**: NeurIPS, ICML, ACL proceedings
4. **Community**: Research communities, GitHub projects
