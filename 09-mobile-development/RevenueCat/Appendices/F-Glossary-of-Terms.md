# Appendix F: Glossary of Terms

## Overview

This appendix provides a comprehensive glossary of terms used throughout the RevenueCat ecosystem and subscription-based app development. Understanding these terms is essential for effectively building, managing, and optimizing subscription apps.

Think of this as your "dictionary" – whenever you encounter an unfamiliar term, reference this glossary for a clear, concise explanation.

---

## A

### A/B Testing
A method of comparing two versions of a paywall, pricing page, or feature to determine which performs better. Users are randomly assigned to different variants, and metrics like conversion rate are measured to identify the winning version.

**Example**: Testing whether "Subscribe Now" or "Start Free Trial" leads to more conversions.

### Active Entitlement
An entitlement that the user currently has access to. For subscriptions, this means the subscription is active and hasn't expired or been cancelled.

### Active Subscriber
A user who has an active subscription. They have paid and maintain access to premium features.

### Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR)
The yearly value of all active subscriptions, normalized to a 12-month period. ARR = MRR × 12.

**Formula**: `ARR = MRR × 12`

### App Store Connect
Apple's platform for managing iOS app distribution, including app submission, product configuration, and analytics. You configure subscriptions and in-app purchases here.

### App User ID
A unique identifier for a user in your app. In RevenueCat, this is used to track subscriptions across devices. If not provided, RevenueCat generates an anonymous ID.

**Best Practice**: Use your own user ID system and pass it to RevenueCat for cross-device synchronization.

### Average Revenue Per Paying User (ARPPU)
The average revenue generated by each paying user. Similar to ARPU but only includes paying users.

**Formula**: `ARPPU = Total Revenue / Number of Paying Users`

### Average Revenue Per User (ARPU)
The average revenue generated per user, including both paying and non-paying users. A key metric for understanding overall revenue efficiency.

**Formula**: `ARPU = Total Revenue / Total Users`

---

## B

### Base Plan
In Google Play Console, a base plan defines the core subscription offering including price, billing period, and renewal type. Multiple offers can be attached to a base plan.

### Billing Issue
A problem with processing a user's payment, such as an expired credit card or insufficient funds. RevenueCat detects billing issues and triggers events.

### Billing Period
The duration of a subscription cycle (e.g., monthly, annual). Determines how often the user is charged.

### Bundle ID
A unique identifier for an iOS app (e.g., `com.company.appname`). Used to link your app to App Store Connect and RevenueCat.

---

## C

### Cancellation
When a user ends their subscription. In the app stores, cancellation means the user has turned off auto-renewal but still has access until the current period ends.

### Churn
The rate at which subscribers cancel their subscriptions. High churn indicates retention problems.

**Formula**: `Churn Rate = (Customers Lost in Period / Customers at Start of Period) × 100`

### Cohort Analysis
A method of analyzing user behavior by grouping users based on a common characteristic or time period (e.g., users who subscribed in January).

**Example**: Comparing retention rates of users who signed up in Q1 vs Q2.

### Consumable
An in-app purchase that can be purchased multiple times (e.g., virtual currency, extra lives). Consumables are not restored.

### Conversion Rate
The percentage of users who complete a desired action, such as purchasing a subscription after viewing the paywall.

**Formula**: `Conversion Rate = (Number of Conversions / Number of Impressions) × 100`

### Customer
A user who has made at least one purchase in your app.

### CustomerInfo
The RevenueCat object containing all information about a user, including active entitlements, subscription status, and purchase history.

### Customer Lifetime Value (CLV or LTV)
The total revenue a user is expected to generate over their entire relationship with your app.

**Formula**: `LTV = Average Revenue Per User × Average Lifetime (in months)`

---

## D

### Decoy Effect
A pricing psychology phenomenon where a third, less attractive option is added to make one of the other options appear more valuable.

**Example**: Adding a $19.99/month option makes the $9.99/month option look like a better deal.

### Developer Account
An account with Apple (Apple Developer Program) or Google (Google Play Console) required to distribute apps on the respective app stores.

### Duration
The length of a subscription period. Common durations: monthly, annual, weekly.

---

## E

### Entitlement
A premium feature or access right that a user unlocks through purchase. Entitlements are defined in RevenueCat and linked to products.

**Example**: `premium_workouts`, `nutrition_tracking`, `personal_trainer`

### Entitlement Info
A RevenueCat object containing detailed information about a specific entitlement, including expiration date, purchase date, and renewal status.

### Event
A specific occurrence in the subscription lifecycle (e.g., purchase, renewal, cancellation). RevenueCat sends webhook events to your backend.

### Experiment
A test designed to determine the impact of a change on user behavior. RevenueCat Experiments allow A/B testing of paywalls and offers.

### Expiration
The date when a subscription ends and the user loses access to premium features (unless it renews).

---

## F

### Free Trial
A promotional period where users can access premium features without paying. After the trial ends, the user is charged (if they don't cancel).

### Feature Gating
The practice of restricting access to premium features based on entitlement status. Users without the required entitlement are shown an upgrade prompt.

---

## G

### Grace Period
An extension period after a subscription expires but before access is revoked. Allows users to update payment methods without losing access.

### Google Play Console
Google's platform for managing Android app distribution, including app submission, product configuration, and analytics.

---

## H

### Healthy Churn
A sustainable churn rate that doesn't negatively impact business growth. Generally considered to be below 5% monthly.

### Hybrid App
An app built with frameworks like React Native or Flutter that allows code sharing across iOS and Android.

---

## I

### Idempotency
A property of operations where multiple identical requests have the same effect as a single request. Important for webhook processing to prevent duplicate events.

### In-App Purchase (IAP)
A purchase made within an app, including subscriptions, consumables, and non-consumables.

### Introductory Offer
A promotional pricing period for new subscribers, such as a discount or free trial. Configured in the app stores.

### iOS App Store
Apple's digital distribution platform for iOS apps.

---

## J

### JSON Web Token (JWT)
A compact, URL-safe token used for authentication. RevenueCat uses JWT for API authentication.

---

## K

### Keystore
An Android file containing cryptographic keys used to sign your app for distribution.

### Key Performance Indicator (KPI)
A measurable value that demonstrates how effectively a company is achieving key business objectives.

**Example KPIs**: MRR, Churn Rate, Conversion Rate, LTV

---

## L

### Lifetime Value (LTV)
See **Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)**.

### Loss Aversion
A cognitive bias where the pain of losing something is more powerful than the pleasure of gaining something. Used in subscription marketing to encourage retention.

---

## M

### MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue)
The predictable monthly revenue generated from all active subscriptions. A key metric for subscription businesses.

**Formula**: `MRR = Sum of All Monthly Subscription Payments`

### Management URL
A URL provided by RevenueCat that allows users to manage their subscription (cancel, update payment method, etc.) in the app store.

### Migration
The process of transferring a user's subscription from one identity to another (e.g., from anonymous to authenticated).

---

## N

### Net Revenue Retention (NRR)
A measure of revenue retention that includes expansion revenue from existing customers.

**Formula**: `NRR = (Revenue from Existing Customers at End of Period / Revenue from Existing Customers at Start of Period) × 100`

### Non-Consumable
An in-app purchase that is purchased once and does not expire (e.g., unlocking a premium feature permanently).

---

## O

### Observer Mode
A RevenueCat configuration where you handle purchase completion manually instead of letting RevenueCat automatically finalize transactions.

### Offering
A group of packages (products) that you present to users. Offerings are configured in RevenueCat and can be changed without an app update.

### Offerings
The RevenueCat object containing all offerings available to a user.

---

## P

### Package
A specific product within an offering. Packages represent the actual products users can purchase (e.g., monthly subscription, annual subscription).

### Package Type
The type of package: `MONTHLY`, `ANNUAL`, `LIFETIME`, `WEEKLY`, or `CUSTOM`.

### Paywall
The user interface screen where users can purchase subscriptions. Designed to convert users to subscribers.

### Period Type
The type of subscription period: `NORMAL` (regular price), `INTRO` (introductory offer), `TRIAL` (free trial).

### Premium Features
Features that are only available to subscribed users. Gated behind entitlements.

### Product
A specific purchasable item from the app store. Products can be subscriptions, consumables, or non-consumables.

### Product ID
The unique identifier for a product in the app store (e.g., `com.fittrackpro.monthly`).

### Promotional Offer
A discount or incentive offered to lapsed subscribers to encourage re-subscription.

### Provisioning Profile
An iOS file that allows your app to be installed on devices and distributed through the App Store.

### Purchase Flow
The sequence of steps a user goes through to complete a purchase, from selecting a plan to transaction completion.

---

## R

### Rate Limiting
The practice of limiting the number of API requests a client can make in a given time period. RevenueCat enforces rate limits to ensure service stability.

### Receipt Validation
The process of verifying that a purchase receipt is legitimate and not fraudulent. RevenueCat handles this automatically.

### Restoration
The process of re-granting entitlements to a user who has previously purchased but is on a new device. Apple requires all subscription apps to support restoration.

### RevenueCat Dashboard
The web interface for managing your RevenueCat project, including products, entitlements, offerings, and analytics.

### Renewal
The automatic renewal of a subscription at the end of a billing period.

### Retention
The ability to keep subscribers over time. High retention is a sign of product value.

**Formula**: `Retention Rate = ((End Subscribers - New Subscribers) / Start Subscribers) × 100`

---

## S

### Sandbox
A testing environment for simulating purchases without using real money. Both Apple and Google provide sandbox testing.

### SDK (Software Development Kit)
A collection of tools and libraries for integrating with a service. RevenueCat provides SDKs for various platforms.

### Secret API Key
A RevenueCat API key used for server-side operations. Starts with `sk_` and must be kept secure.

### Service Account
A Google Cloud account used by RevenueCat to access your Google Play Console for receipt validation.

### SKU (Stock Keeping Unit)
A unique identifier for a product. In app stores, SKU is often used interchangeably with Product ID.

### Subscriber
A user with an active subscription.

### Subscription
A recurring payment model where users pay periodically for access to premium features.

### Subscription Group (iOS)
A group of related subscription products (e.g., monthly and annual versions of the same service). Users can upgrade/downgrade within a group.

---

## T

### Test Store
RevenueCat's built-in testing environment that doesn't require connecting to real app stores. Allows rapid testing of purchase flows.

### Trial Conversion Rate
The percentage of free trial users who convert to paid subscribers.

**Formula**: `Trial Conversion Rate = (Number of Paid Conversions / Number of Trial Starts) × 100`

### Turnover
The rate at which customers cancel or leave. High turnover (churn) is problematic.

---

## U

### Upgrade
When a user moves to a higher-tier subscription (e.g., from monthly to annual or basic to premium).

### User ID
See **App User ID**.

---

## V

### Virtual Currency
An in-app currency that users can purchase and spend within the app. RevenueCat supports virtual currency management.

### Volume (API)
The number of API requests made in a given time period.

---

## W

### Webhook
An HTTP callback that RevenueCat sends to your server when subscription events occur (e.g., purchase, renewal, cancellation).

### Webhook Secret
A secret key used to verify the authenticity of webhook requests from RevenueCat.

### Win-Back Campaign
A marketing campaign targeting lapsed subscribers with offers to re-subscribe.

---

## X

### Xcode
Apple's integrated development environment (IDE) for building iOS apps.

---

## Y

### Yearly Revenue
The total revenue generated over a 12-month period.

---

## Z

### Zero-Day
A user who subscribes on their first day of using the app. High zero-day conversion rates indicate effective onboarding.

---

## Summary

This glossary covers the essential terms you'll encounter when building subscription-based applications with RevenueCat:

1. **Core Concepts**: Entitlements, offerings, packages, products
2. **Business Metrics**: MRR, ARPU, LTV, churn, conversion
3. **Technical Terms**: SDK, API, webhook, sandbox, receipt validation
4. **Platform Terms**: App Store Connect, Google Play Console, bundle ID
5. **User Terms**: Subscriber, customer, free trial, grace period

### Quick Reference

- **API Key**: `app_` for client, `sk_` for server
- **Product ID**: Unique identifier in app stores
- **App User ID**: Your user's ID in RevenueCat
- **Entitlement**: Premium feature access
- **Offering**: Group of packages to display
- **Package**: Individual product with price
