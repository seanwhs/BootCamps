# Appendix G: Sample App Store Copy & Guidelines

## Overview

This appendix provides ready-to-use copy templates for your app store listings, along with guidelines for creating effective app store metadata. Good app store copy is essential for discoverability and conversion – it's often the first thing potential users see.

Think of this as your "copywriting toolkit" – templates you can adapt for your specific app, along with best practices for each element of your app store listing.

---

## App Store Optimization (ASO) Guidelines

### Key Concepts

| Element | Impact | Best Practice |
|---------|--------|---------------|
| App Name | High | Include keywords, keep under 30 characters |
| Subtitle | High | 30 characters, describe key benefit |
| Description | Medium | First 3-4 lines are most important |
| Keywords | High | 100 characters, research relevant terms |
| Screenshots | Very High | Show key features, include text overlays |
| Preview Video | High | 15-30 seconds, show app in action |

### Keyword Research Tips

1. **Use App Store Search**: Type relevant terms and see what autocomplete suggests
2. **Analyze Competitors**: See what keywords successful competitors are targeting
3. **Long-Tail Keywords**: Target specific phrases (e.g., "workout tracker for runners")
4. **Localization**: Research keywords in different languages for international markets

## App Store Connect (iOS) Templates

### App Name

**Template:**
```
[Brand] - [Key Benefit/Feature]
```

**Examples:**
- "FitTrack Pro - Workout Tracker"
- "FitTrack Pro - Personal Trainer"
- "FitTrack Pro - Nutrition & Fitness"

**Best Practices:**
- Keep under 30 characters
- Include primary keyword
- Make it memorable
- Don't use generic terms ("App", "Mobile")

### Subtitle

**Template:**
```
[Key Feature] + [Secondary Feature] + [User Benefit]
```

**Examples:**
- "Track workouts, nutrition, and progress"
- "Personal training & meal planning"
- "500+ exercises & nutrition tracking"

**Best Practices:**
- Keep under 30 characters
- Include key benefits
- Use action verbs
- Appeal to user needs

### Description Template

**Template:**
```
[Open with compelling hook]
[Describe key features (3-5 bullet points)]
[Explain benefits]
[Call to action]
[Technical information]
```

**Example:**

```markdown
Transform your fitness journey with FitTrack Pro - the all-in-one workout and nutrition app.

**🏋️ Premium Workouts**
Access 500+ exercises and custom workout plans designed by certified trainers. From HIIT to yoga, find the perfect workout for your goals.

**🥗 Nutrition Tracking**
Log meals, track macros, and get personalized meal recommendations. Understand exactly what you're eating and how it impacts your fitness.

**💬 Personal Trainer Chat**
Get 1-on-1 guidance from certified trainers. Ask questions, get form tips, and stay motivated.

**📊 Progress Analytics**
Visualize your progress with detailed charts and insights. See how your fitness is improving over time.

**✨ Premium Features Available**
- Unlimited workout creation
- Advanced analytics
- Personal trainer access
- Custom meal plans

Download FitTrack Pro today and start your transformation!

Privacy Policy: [URL]
Terms of Service: [URL]
```

### Keywords

**Template:**
```
[Primary Keyword], [Secondary Keyword], [Category Keyword], [Feature Keyword], [Long-tail Phrase]
```

**Example:**
```
workout tracker, fitness app, exercise trainer, nutrition log, personal training, meal planner, HIIT workouts, yoga app, gym companion
```

**Best Practices:**
- Use all 100 characters
- Separate keywords with commas
- Include primary keywords first
- Don't repeat keywords
- Test different combinations

### Screenshot Guidelines

**Requirements:**
- 5.5-inch display: 1242 x 2208 pixels
- 6.5-inch display: 1284 x 2778 pixels
- JPEG or PNG format
- No overlay text beyond 1/3 of screen

**Best Practices:**
1. **Show the Paywall**: Include a screenshot of your subscription screen
2. **Highlight Features**: Show premium features in action
3. **Include Text Overlays**: Explain what users are seeing
4. **Show Value**: Demonstrate the benefit of subscribing
5. **Test Variations**: A/B test different screenshot sequences

**Sample Screenshot Caption Template:**
```
[Action] + [Benefit] + [Emoji/Icon]
```

**Examples:**
- "💪 500+ Premium Workouts | Get stronger"
- "🥗 Track Your Nutrition | Know exactly what you eat"
- "💬 Chat with Trainers | Get personalized guidance"

## Google Play Store (Android) Templates

**Template:**
```
[Brand] - [Key Benefit/Feature]
```

**Examples:**
- "FitTrack Pro - Workout & Nutrition"
- "FitTrack Pro - Personal Trainer"
- "FitTrack Pro - Fitness Tracker"

**Best Practices:**

- Include primary keyword
- Make it searchable

### Short Description

**Template:**
```
[Key Benefit] + [Key Feature] + [Key Feature]
```

**Examples:**
- "Track workouts, nutrition, and progress with personalized training plans."
- "Get 500+ workouts, nutrition tracking, and 1-on-1 trainer chat."

**Best Practices:**
- Keep under 80 characters
- Include primary keywords
- Describe core value proposition

### Full Description Template

```markdown
[Headline with primary benefit]

[Key Feature 1 with description]
[Key Feature 2 with description]
[Key Feature 3 with description]

[Premium features section]
[What users are saying (testimonials)]

- Updated: [Date]
```

**Requirements:**
- 1024 x 500 pixels
- PNG or JPEG
- No more than 2 lines of text

**Best Practices:**
- Show app in action
- Use consistent branding
- Include key benefit
- Make it visually appealing

## In-App Copy Guidelines

### Paywall Copy

#### Headline Templates

| Type | Template | Example |
|------|----------|---------|
| Problem-Solution | "Stop [pain point], start [benefit]" | "Stop guessing, start seeing results" |
| Value Proposition | "Get [benefit] with [feature]" | "Get personalized fitness with AI training" |
| Curiosity | "The [number] [benefit] you need" | "The 5 features you've been missing" |
| Urgency | "[Offer] ends in [time]" | "Limited offer ends in 24 hours" |

#### Feature Description Templates

```markdown
**[Feature Name]**: [One-sentence description] + [Specific benefit]

Examples:
🏋️ Premium Workouts: Access 500+ exercises with video demonstrations and custom routines. Get stronger, faster.

🥗 Nutrition Tracking: Log meals, track macros, and get personalized recommendations. Know exactly what fuels your body.

💬 Personal Trainer: Chat with certified trainers anytime. Get form tips, motivation, and guidance.
```

#### Pricing Display Templates

```markdown
**Monthly Plan**
[Price]/month
[Key benefit 1]
[Key benefit 2]
[Key benefit 3]
[CTA: Start Free Trial]

**Annual Plan**
[Price]/year (Save [percentage]%)
[Key benefit 1]
[Key benefit 2]
[Key benefit 3]
[Bonus benefit]
[CTA: Subscribe & Save]
```

#### Trust Signals

```markdown
🔒 Secure payment
🔄 Cancel anytime
⭐ 4.8/5 from 50,000+ users
📱 App Store Featured
```

**Subject:** We're sorry to see you go 😢

```markdown
[Reactivate Button]

If you'd like to share feedback about why you cancelled, we'd love to hear it:

[Feedback Survey Button]

We hope to see you again soon,
The [App Name] Team
```

#### Grace Period Email

**Subject:** ⚠️ Your subscription will end soon - Update your payment

```markdown
Hi [Name],

We noticed an issue with your payment. Your subscription will end in [number] days if we can't process it.

**What's happening:**
- Payment method: [Payment type] ending in [last 4]
- Issue: [Description of issue]
- Grace period ends: [Date]

**What you need to do:**
Update your payment method to keep your access.

[Update Payment Button]

If you don't update your payment, you'll lose access to:
- Premium workouts
- Nutrition tracking
- Personal trainer chat
- Progress analytics

Your data will be saved for 30 days.

Need help? Contact us at support@[appname].com

The [App Name] Team
```

## Summary

This appendix provides ready-to-use templates and guidelines for:

1. **App Store Copy**: Name, subtitle, description, keywords
2. **Google Play Copy**: Title, short description, full description
3. **In-App Copy**: Paywall headlines, features, pricing, trust signals
4. **Email Templates**: Welcome, cancellation, win-back, grace period
5. **Review Guidelines**: iOS and Android compliance checklists
6. **Localization**: Key phrases for international markets

### Key Takeaways

1. **ASO is Critical**: Good app store copy drives discoverability
2. **First 3-4 Lines Matter**: Most users don't read the full description
3. **Screenshots Convert**: Show value, don't just show screens
4. **Be Compliant**: Follow app store guidelines
5. **Test and Iterate**: Continuously optimize based on data
