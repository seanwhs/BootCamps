# Part 6 – Capstone Project: Enterprise Business Analysis Simulation

---

## Welcome to the Capstone Project

Congratulations on completing all five foundational phases of the Master Business Analysis series. You have acquired the full toolkit of a professional Business Analyst—from strategic analysis and requirements engineering to business modeling, solution evaluation, and advanced practices like Agile, data-driven analysis, AI assistance, and enterprise-level thinking.

Now, it is time to apply everything you have learned in a realistic, end-to-end enterprise transformation simulation. This capstone project will guide you through a complete Business Analysis lifecycle, from a vague organizational problem to a comprehensive, executive-ready portfolio of professional BA artifacts.

---

## Capstone Overview

### The Scenario: "SmartCare Health Platform"

**Organization:** MediConnect Healthcare Network (the same organization you have been analyzing throughout the series)

**The Problem:** MediConnect is a mid-sized healthcare provider network with 250 employees, 5 clinic locations, and 45,000 active patients. They are struggling with:

- **Disconnected legacy systems** (EHR, scheduling, billing, patient portal, lab, inventory, reporting) that do not communicate
- **Declining patient satisfaction** (down 15% in 18 months)
- **Clinician burnout** (40% of time spent on administrative work)
- **Rising IT costs** (30% above industry average)
- **Market share loss** to more digitally advanced competitors

**The Opportunity:** Transform MediConnect into a modern, integrated, patient-centric digital health platform that improves patient outcomes, reduces costs, enhances clinician satisfaction, and positions the organization for future growth.

**Your Role:** You are the Lead Business Analyst on this transformation initiative. You will guide the project through all six phases of the BA lifecycle, producing a complete professional portfolio.

**Project Parameters:**
- **Budget:** $5-8 million
- **Timeline:** 18 months
- **Team Size:** 40+ people (BAs, developers, QA, project management, change management)
- **Stakeholders:** Executives, clinicians, administrative staff, patients, IT teams, regulators, partners

### Capstone Structure

The capstone is divided into **six phases**, each corresponding to a major stage of the Business Analysis lifecycle:

| Phase | Focus | Key Deliverables |
|-------|-------|------------------|
| **Phase 1: Business Discovery** | Problem analysis, strategy alignment, stakeholder identification | Strategic Analysis Report, Stakeholder Register, Business Case |
| **Phase 2: Requirements Engineering** | Elicitation, analysis, and documentation | BRD, Product Backlog, User Stories, Use Cases, RTM |
| **Phase 3: Business Modeling** | Process modeling, data modeling, solution design | BPMN Models, ERD, DFD, Wireframes, Sequence Diagrams |
| **Phase 4: Solution Evaluation** | Feasibility, cost-benefit, vendor evaluation, PoC | Feasibility Study, CBA, Vendor Evaluation, PoC Plan |
| **Phase 5: Delivery Support** | Testing, UAT, change management, training | UAT Plan, Test Scripts, Defect Log, Training Plan, Change Management Plan |
| **Phase 6: Final Presentation** | Compilation and presentation of the complete portfolio | Executive Summary, Complete BA Portfolio, Presentation Deck |
