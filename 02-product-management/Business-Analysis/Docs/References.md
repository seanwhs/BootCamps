# References and Resources
## Master Business Analysis: From Strategy to Execution

---

### About This Guide

This appendix provides a comprehensive list of references, resources, and recommended reading for the "Master Business Analysis: From Strategy to Execution" series. It is organized by category to support ongoing learning, certification preparation, and professional development.

**How to Use This Guide:**
- **Core References:** Essential texts for BA practice
- **Certification Resources:** Materials for IIBA and PMI certification
- **Advanced Topics:** Resources for specialized BA areas
- **Tools and Templates:** Software and practical resources
- **Professional Development:** Communities, events, and continuing education

### 17.3 Requirements Types Quick Card

| Type | Description | Example |
|------|-------------|---------|
| Business Requirement | High-level organizational goal | "Increase patient satisfaction" |
| Stakeholder Requirement | Need of specific stakeholder group | "Patients need online booking" |
| Functional Requirement | What the system must do | "System shall allow online booking" |
| Non-Functional Requirement | How the system performs | "System response time < 2 seconds" |
| Transition Requirement | Temporary requirement | "Migrate legacy data" |
| Business Rule | Policy or decision | "Verify insurance before service" |
