# Tools, Artifacts, and Models Reference
## Master Business Analysis: From Strategy to Execution

---

### About This Guide

This appendix provides a comprehensive reference guide to the tools, artifacts, and models used throughout the Master Business Analysis series. It is organized as a quick-reference catalog for practicing BAs, trainers, and students.

**How to Use This Guide:**
- **Tools:** Software and techniques for BA work
- **Artifacts:** Documents and deliverables
- **Models:** Visual representations and diagrams
- **Templates:** Ready-to-use formats
- **Techniques:** Methods and approaches
- **Frameworks:** Structured approaches to analysis
