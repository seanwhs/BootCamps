### Quiz 1.1: Understanding Business Analysis (Module 1)

**Instructions:** Select the best answer for each question.

---

**1. Which of the following BEST defines Business Analysis?**
- A) Writing technical specifications for developers
- B) Managing project timelines and budgets
- C) Enabling change by defining needs and recommending solutions that deliver value
- D) Testing software to ensure quality

**Answer: C**

*Explanation: Business Analysis is the practice of enabling change in an organization by defining needs and recommending solutions that deliver value to stakeholders. This is the BABOK® Guide definition.*

---

**2. A Business Analyst is primarily responsible for:**
- A) Ensuring the project stays on budget
- B) Deciding which features to build
- C) Ensuring the team is building the right thing
- D) Writing all the code

**Answer: C**

*Explanation: The BA's primary responsibility is ensuring the team builds the right thing (validation), while the PM ensures it's built right (verification), and the PO decides what to build next.*

---

**3. Which of the following is NOT a core competency of a modern Business Analyst?**
- A) Active listening
- B) Process modeling
- C) Code compilation
- D) Stakeholder facilitation

**Answer: C**

*Explanation: Code compilation is a development activity, not a core BA competency. BAs need communication, modeling, facilitation, and analytical skills.*

---

**4. According to the BABOK® Guide, how many knowledge areas are there?**
- A) 4
- B) 6
- C) 8
- D) 10

**Answer: B**

*Explanation: The BABOK® Guide defines 6 knowledge areas: (1) Business Analysis Planning and Monitoring, (2) Elicitation and Collaboration, (3) Requirements Lifecycle Management, (4) Strategy Analysis, (5) Requirements Analysis and Design Definition, and (6) Solution Evaluation.*

---

**5. The difference between a Business Analyst and a Project Manager is that:**

- B) The BA focuses on "how" and the PM focuses on "what"

- A) Elicitation and Analysis
- B) Strategy Analysis

- A) Identify stakeholders

- C) Prioritize requirements

- A) Jeff Patton
