### Case Study 1: Financial Services – "QuickPay" Digital Transformation

**Industry:** Banking/Financial Services  
**Organization Size:** 5,000+ employees, 2 million customers  
**Project Type:** Digital Transformation  

---

#### The Situation

QuickPay Financial Services is a regional bank with 200 branches across three states. They have been losing market share to digital-first competitors like fintech startups and neobanks. Their customer base is aging, and they struggle to attract younger customers.

**Current Challenges:**
- Legacy core banking system from the 1990s
- Mobile app has a 2.3-star rating and is rarely updated
- Customers must visit branches for most transactions
- 45% of customer service calls are about basic account inquiries
- IT costs are 25% higher than industry average

**Stakeholder Quotes:**

**CEO:** "We need to become a digital-first bank within 24 months. Our survival depends on it."

**Head of Retail Banking:** "Our branches are our strength. We can't just close them—we need to transform them."

**Head of IT:** "We're running on 30-year-old COBOL code. We need to modernize, but we can't afford to disrupt operations."

**Customer (age 25):** "I never go to the branch. I want to do everything from my phone."

**Customer (age 65):** "I like talking to a person. I'm nervous about doing everything online."

- Mobile adoption: 30% → 75% of customers

2. Launch new mobile app with MVP features first
3. Transform branches into advisory centers
4. Provide extensive digital training for staff
5. Implement robust change management
