### Case Study 1: Financial Services – "QuickPay" Digital Transformation

**Industry:** Banking/Financial Services  
**Organization Size:** 5,000+ employees, 2 million customers  
**Project Type:** Digital Transformation  

---

#### The Situation

QuickPay Financial Services is a regional bank with 200 branches across three states. They have been losing market share to digital-first competitors like fintech startups and neobanks. Their customer base is aging, and they struggle to attract younger customers.

**Current Challenges:**
- Legacy core banking system from the 1990s
- Mobile app has a 2.3-star rating and is rarely updated
- Customers must visit branches for most transactions
- 45% of customer service calls are about basic account inquiries
- IT costs are 25% higher than industry average

**Stakeholder Quotes:**

**CEO:** "We need to become a digital-first bank within 24 months. Our survival depends on it."

**Head of Retail Banking:** "Our branches are our strength. We can't just close them—we need to transform them."

**Head of IT:** "We're running on 30-year-old COBOL code. We need to modernize, but we can't afford to disrupt operations."

**Customer (age 25):** "I never go to the branch. I want to do everything from my phone."

**Customer (age 65):** "I like talking to a person. I'm nervous about doing everything online."

---

#### Suggested Solution Highlights

**Key Requirements:**
- Must Have: Mobile account opening, mobile check deposit, mobile transfers
- Should Have: Push notifications, budgeting tools, card controls
- Could Have: Investment management, credit score monitoring
- Won't Have: Full branch replacement

**Key Metrics:**
- Mobile app rating: 2.3 → 4.5 stars
- Mobile adoption: 30% → 75% of customers
- Branch visits: Decrease 40%
- Customer satisfaction: Improve 25%
- IT costs: Reduce 20%

**Key Recommendations:**
1. Modernize core banking system in phases (not a big bang)
2. Launch new mobile app with MVP features first
3. Transform branches into advisory centers
4. Provide extensive digital training for staff
5. Implement robust change management

### Case Study 3: Retail – "UrbanWear" Omnichannel Transformation

**Industry:** Retail/Apparel  
**Organization Size:** 10,000 employees, 500 stores  
**Project Type:** Omnichannel Retail  

---

#### The Situation

UrbanWear is a mid-market apparel retailer with 500 stores across North America. They have a strong brand presence but are struggling with declining sales and customer loyalty.

**Current Challenges:**
- Online sales are only 15% of revenue (vs. 30%+ for competitors)
- Inventory systems don't sync across online and stores
- Customers can't check store availability online
- Returns are handled poorly (long wait times, confusing process)
- Loyalty program has low engagement
- Store associates lack visibility into online orders

**Stakeholder Quotes:**

**CEO:** "We need to be where our customers are—online, in-store, and everywhere in between."

**Head of E-Commerce:** "We need a unified platform. Our current systems don't talk to each other."

**Store Manager:** "Customers come in asking about items they saw online, and we can't help them."

**Head of Supply Chain:** "Our inventory data is 48 hours behind. We can't make real-time decisions."

**Customer:** "I want to buy online and pick up in store. I want to check if something is available before I drive there."

---

#### Your Tasks

**Task 1: Strategic Analysis**

- Complete Porter's Five Forces
- Develop a Business Model Canvas for UrbanWear
- Define OKRs for the omnichannel transformation

**Task 2: Requirements**

- Write 15 user stories for omnichannel features
- Create use cases for: buy online/pickup in-store, check store availability, unified returns
- Identify non-functional requirements for real-time inventory

**Task 3: Business Modeling**

- BPMN model: Current returns process
- BPMN model: Future returns process
- Value Stream Map: Inventory management
- ERD: Inventory, Orders, Store, Customer

**Task 4: Evaluation**

- Cost-benefit analysis (assume $10M investment, $5M annual benefits)
- Vendor evaluation for e-commerce platform
- Feasibility assessment

### Case Study 4: Government – "CitizenConnect" Digital Services

**Industry:** Government/Public Sector  
**Organization Size:** 1,000 employees, 500,000 citizens  
**Project Type:** Digital Government  

---

#### The Situation

CitizenConnect is a state government agency responsible for social services. They provide benefits, licenses, and support to citizens, but their processes are outdated and frustrating.

**Current Challenges:**
- Most services require in-person visits
- Application processes take 4-6 weeks
- Citizens have no online visibility into application status
- Staff spend 70% of time on administrative tasks
- Multiple systems don't communicate

**Stakeholder Quotes:**

**Director:** "Citizens are frustrated. We need to modernize, but we have budget constraints and regulatory requirements."

**Staff Member:** "I spend most of my day entering data from paper forms into the system. It's soul-crushing."

**Citizen:** "I had to take a day off work just to apply for a benefit. I waited for 3 hours. This is unacceptable."

**Legislator:** "We need accountability and transparency. We need to show citizens we're using their tax dollars effectively."

**IT Director:** "We're running on 20-year-old systems. We need modernization, but we can't afford any downtime."

---

#### Your Tasks

**Task 1: Strategic Analysis**

- Complete PESTLE analysis (consider political/regulatory factors)
- Identify key performance indicators (KPIs)
- Define strategic objectives

**Task 2: Stakeholder Analysis**

- Identify all stakeholder groups (including citizens, legislators, advocacy groups)
- Create an influence-attitude matrix
- Develop personas for: a frustrated citizen, a dedicated staff member, the IT Director

**Task 3: Requirements**

- Write 20 user stories covering: online applications, status tracking, document upload
- Identify compliance and regulatory requirements
- Create use cases for: benefit application, status inquiry, document submission

**Task 4: Solution Evaluation**

- Gap analysis
- Feasibility assessment (consider budget constraints)
- Cost-benefit analysis
- Build vs. buy recommendation

**Task 5: Change Management**

- Readiness assessment
- Communication plan for citizens
- Training plan for staff
- Resistance management

### Case Study 5: Technology – "CodeFlow" Platform Modernization

**Industry:** Technology/Software  
**Organization Size:** 500 employees, 5,000 enterprise customers  
**Project Type:** Platform Modernization  

---

#### The Situation

CodeFlow is a software company providing enterprise project management tools. Their platform was built 15 years ago and struggles to compete with modern, AI-powered competitors.

**Current Challenges:**
- Platform is outdated and difficult to maintain
- Competitors have AI-powered features
- Customers are churning (18% annually)
- Development velocity is slow (takes 3 months to deploy)
- Technical debt is overwhelming

**Stakeholder Quotes:**

**CEO:** "Our market share is declining. We need to innovate, fast."

**Chief Product Officer:** "Our customers are asking for AI features. We can't deliver with our current architecture."

**Lead Developer:** "The codebase is a nightmare. We need to refactor, but management wants new features."

**CTO:** "We need to modernize. But we can't stop development while we do it."

**Enterprise Customer:** "Your platform doesn't keep up with our needs. We're evaluating alternatives."

**SMB Customer:** "Your platform is too complex and expensive for our needs."

---

#### Your Tasks

**Task 1: Strategy Analysis**

- SWOT analysis
- Porter's Five Forces
- Value Chain Analysis
- Competitive Analysis (list 3-5 competitors)

**Task 2: Product Requirements**

- 15 user stories for the modernized platform
- 10 functional requirements for AI features
- Non-functional requirements: performance, scalability, security

**Task 3: Business Modeling**

- Value Stream Map: Feature delivery process
- Current vs. future development process
- Data model for enterprise project management

**Task 4: Evaluation**

- Cost-benefit analysis for modernization
- Build vs. buy vs. partner decision
- Migration strategy for customers
- Risk assessment

**Task 5: Change Management**

- Communication plan for customers
- Migration support strategy
- Training plan for sales and support teams
- Adoption metrics
