### Executive Summary

```
EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

PROJECT OVERVIEW:
MediConnect Healthcare Network is transforming its operations through the
SmartCare Integrated Health Platform. This $6.5 million initiative will
replace disconnected legacy systems with a modern, integrated platform
that improves patient experience, clinician efficiency, and operational
excellence.

KEY FINDINGS:
- MediConnect faces declining patient satisfaction (72%, down 15% in 18 months)
- Clinicians spend 40% of time on administrative tasks
- IT costs are 30% above industry average
- 7 disconnected systems create data silos and inefficiencies
- Patient attrition is 15% annually

RECOMMENDATIONS:
1. Implement integrated digital health platform
2. Modernize clinical and operational workflows
3. Enable digital patient engagement (portal, online booking)
4. Transform data management and analytics

BUSINESS CASE:
- Investment: $6.5 million
- 5-Year Net Benefit: $11.7 million
- ROI: 180%
- Payback Period: 2.9 years

VALUE PROPOSITION:
The SmartCare platform will:
- Improve patient satisfaction from 72% to 85%
- Reduce clinician admin time from 15 to 5 minutes per visit
- Lower IT costs from 5.5% to 4.0% of revenue
- Reduce patient attrition from 15% to 8%
- Achieve 95%+ data accuracy across all systems

IMPLEMENTATION ROADMAP:
- Phase 1 (Months 1-6): Foundation and core platform
- Phase 2 (Months 7-12): Integration and expansion
- Phase 3 (Months 13-18): Optimization and full deployment

READINESS: GO
The project is recommended for approval based on strong business case,
feasibility, and stakeholder alignment.
```

### Requirements Package (Condensed)

```
BUSINESS REQUIREMENTS

BR-001: Improve patient satisfaction
  Description: Increase patient satisfaction scores from 72% to 85%
  Rationale: Declining satisfaction has resulted in 15% patient attrition
  Priority: Critical

BR-002: Reduce clinician administrative burden
  Description: Decrease clinician admin time from 15 to 5 minutes per visit
  Rationale: Clinician burnout is increasing and affecting retention
  Priority: Critical

BR-003: Improve operational efficiency
  Description: Reduce scheduling-related manual coordination time by 30%
  Rationale: Scheduling inefficiency is driving operational costs
  Priority: High

FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS (Partial)

FR-001: Patient Scheduling
  FR-001.1: System shall allow patients to view available slots online
  FR-001.2: System shall allow patients to book appointments online
  FR-001.3: System shall allow patients to cancel appointments online
  FR-001.4: System shall allow patients to reschedule appointments online
  FR-001.5: System shall send confirmation after booking
  FR-001.6: System shall send reminder 24 hours before appointment

FR-002: Clinical Documentation
  FR-002.1: System shall capture and store patient demographics
  FR-002.2: System shall capture medical history and medications
  FR-002.3: System shall store past visits and treatment records
  FR-002.4: System shall allow voice-to-text documentation
  FR-002.5: System shall store and display lab results

NON-FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS (Partial)

NFR-001: Performance - System response time < 2 seconds
NFR-002: Performance - Support 500+ concurrent users
NFR-003: Security - HIPAA-compliant security
NFR-004: Security - Role-based access control
NFR-005: Availability - 99.9% uptime SLA
NFR-006: Scalability - Support 50% growth in users and data

USER STORIES (Partial)

US-001: As a patient, I want to view available appointment slots online,
       so that I can choose a time that works for me.
  Acceptance Criteria:
  1. Search by date, provider, clinic
  2. View available slots with provider and location
  3. Slots update in real-time

US-002: As a patient, I want to book appointments online,
       so that I can schedule care without calling.
  Acceptance Criteria:
  1. Select available time
  2. Confirm appointment details
  3. Receive confirmation email/SMS

US-003: As a clinician, I want to view complete patient history in one view,
       so that I can make informed clinical decisions.
  Acceptance Criteria:
  1. View includes: demographics, meds, allergies, past visits, lab results
  2. Data organized chronologically
  3. Load time < 2 seconds
```

### Business Models (Condensed)

```
BPMN AS-IS PROCESS: Patient Scheduling

POOL: Patient
[Patient Needs Appointment] → [Calls Clinic] → [Waits for Callback]

POOL: Clinic (Front Desk Lane)
[Answers Call] → [Searches Availability (multiple systems)] →
[Provides Options] → [Gateway: Availability?] →
[If No: Suggests Alternative] →
[If Yes: Confirms Appointment] → [Enters into System(s)] →
[Sends Confirmation]

POOL: Clinic (Provider Lane)
[Reviews Schedule]

PAIN POINTS:
- Manual search across multiple systems (10+ min)
- Phone-only scheduling
- No patient self-service
- Data entry errors
- No confirmation automation

BPMN TO-BE PROCESS: Patient Scheduling

POOL: Patient
[Patient Needs Appointment] → [Logs into Portal/App] →
[Selects Date/Time] → [Provides Required Info] →
[Gateway: Insurance Verified?] → [If Yes: Confirms and Books] →
[End: Appointment Booked]

POOL: Clinic System (Automated)
[Check Provider Availability] → [Check Resource Availability] →
[Find Best Match] → [Verify Insurance] →
[Process Booking] → [Send Confirmation] →
[Update Schedules] → [Send Reminder]

ERD: Core Entities

PATIENT (PatientID, FirstName, LastName, DOB, Gender, Phone, Email, Address)
  ↓ 1
  ↓ m
APPOINTMENT (AppointmentID, PatientID, ProviderID, Date, Time, Status, Type)
  ↓ 1
  ↓ m
MEDICALRECORD (RecordID, AppointmentID, PatientID, Diagnosis, Treatment, Notes)
  ↓ 1
  ↓ m
PRESCRIPTION (PrescriptionID, PatientID, ProviderID, Medication, Dosage, Frequency)
  ↓ 1
  ↓ m
LABRESULT (LabResultID, PatientID, ProviderID, TestName, Result, ReferenceRange)

PROVIDER (ProviderID, FirstName, LastName, Specialty, LicenseNumber)
  ↓ 1
  ↓ m
CLAIM (ClaimID, PatientID, ProviderID, ServiceDate, Amount, Status, PaidDate)

INSURANCE (InsuranceID, PatientID, ProviderName, PolicyNumber, EffectiveDate)
```
