# Appendix D: Comprehensive Templates and Checklists Library

This appendix provides a complete, ready-to-use library of professional BA templates and checklists. Each template is designed to be copied, adapted, and used in real projects. All templates follow industry best practices and BABOK® Guide standards.

### D.3.2 Interview Guide Template

```
INTERVIEW GUIDE

INTERVIEWEE: [Name]
TITLE/ROLE: [Title]
DEPARTMENT: [Department]
DATE/TIME: [Date, Time]
DURATION: [Minutes]
INTERVIEWER(S): [Names]
OBJECTIVE: [What we need to learn]

PRE-INTERVIEW NOTES:
- Background: [Relevant background]
- Current Process: [Brief description]
- Known Pain Points: [List]
- Key Questions: [Important questions to cover]

WARM-UP QUESTIONS (5 min):
1. "Tell me about your role and how long you've been here."
2. "What does a typical day look like for you?"
3. "What do you enjoy most about your work?"

CORE QUESTIONS (30-40 min):
4. "What are the biggest challenges you face in your role?"
5. "What tools/systems do you use daily?"
6. "What frustrates you most about the current systems/processes?"
7. "What workarounds have you developed?"
8. "What information do you need that you don't have access to?"
9. "If you could change one thing, what would it be?"
10. "How do you currently handle [specific process/task]?"
11. "What does an ideal solution look like to you?"
12. "What would help you be more effective?"

DEEPER PROBES (10-15 min):
13. "Can you tell me more about that?"
14. "Can you give me a specific example?"
15. "What makes that particularly challenging?"
16. "What do you mean by [term]?"
17. "How did you handle that situation?"

CLOSING (5-10 min):
18. "Is there anything I haven't asked that you think is important?"
19. "Who else should we talk to?"
20. "Would you be open to a follow-up conversation if needed?"

POST-INTERVIEW NOTES:
- Key Insights:
- Pain Points Identified:
- Requirements Implied:
- Follow-up Questions:
- Additional Stakeholders:
- Next Steps:

INTERVIEWEE SIGN-OFF (Optional):
[Signature] [Date]
```

```
| 2:20 | 20 min | Summary & Next Steps | | Facilitator |
| 2:40 | 5 min | Close | | Facilitator |

5. GROUND RULES
   - One conversation at a time
   - Build on each other's ideas
   - Stay focused on the topic
   - All ideas are valid (no bad ideas)
   - Be respectful of all perspectives
   - Phones/laptops off (except for activities)
   - Start and end on time

6. MATERIALS NEEDED
   - Whiteboard or flip charts
   - Markers (various colors)
   - Sticky notes (various colors)
   - Handouts
   - Projector/screen
   - Recording equipment (with permission)
   - Name tags/tents
   - Templates
   - Refreshments

7. FACILITATION TECHNIQUES
   - Brainstorming: Generate ideas, no judgment
   - Dot voting: Prioritization
   - Affinity grouping: Organize themes
   - Round robin: Ensure all voices heard
   - Parking lot: Capture off-topic ideas
   - Timeboxing: Stay on schedule
   - Silent brainstorming: Ideas individually first

8. EXIT CRITERIA
   - Clear understanding of [topic]
   - Identified [outputs]
   - Action items defined
   - Next steps agreed

9. FOLLOW-UP
   - Action Item 1: [Description, Owner, Due Date]
```

```
   - Group dynamics
   - Consensus or disagreement
   - Emotional responses
   - Unstated concerns
```

```
- DECIMAL(p,s): Decimal number (p=precision, s=scale)
- VARCHAR(n): Variable-length string (n=max length)
- CHAR(n): Fixed-length string (n=length)
- DATE: Date (YYYY-MM-DD)
- DATETIME: Date and time (YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS)
- TIMESTAMP: Timestamp with timezone
- BOOLEAN: True/False
- TEXT: Large text (unlimited)

VALIDATION RULE EXAMPLES:
- NOT NULL: Cannot be empty
- UNIQUE: Must be unique across records
- CHECK: Check condition
- FORMAT: Pattern matching
- RANGE: Within range
- REFERENCE: Must exist in referenced table
```
