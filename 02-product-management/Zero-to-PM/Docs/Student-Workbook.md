# Student Workbook: The Zero-to-One Product Manager

## Your Companion to the Complete Series

---

## How to Use This Workbook

This workbook is designed to accompany the 10-part "Zero-to-One Product Manager" series. For each part, you'll find:

1. **Key Concepts** - A quick summary of what you learned
2. **Exercises** - Hands-on activities to apply your learning
3. **Reflection Questions** - Deepen your understanding through writing
4. **Portfolio Tracker** - Check off what you've completed
5. **Self-Assessment** - Rate your confidence in each skill

**How to use this workbook:**
- Complete it alongside each part of the series
- Fill in your answers directly in the workbook
- Use it as a study guide for interviews
- Keep it as a reference for your PM journey

## Appendix: Interview Prep Cheat Sheet

### Key Frameworks

| Framework | Formula/Mnemonic |
|-----------|------------------|
| **RICE** | Reach × Impact × Confidence ÷ Effort |
| **Kano** | Basic, Performance, Excitement |
| **JTBD** | "What job is the user hiring?" |
| **STAR** | Situation, Task, Action, Result |
| **AARRR** | Acquisition, Activation, Retention, Referral, Revenue |
| **Growth Pyramid** | Retention → Activation → Acquisition → Revenue |

### Key Metrics

| Metric | Definition |
|--------|------------|
| **NPS** | Promoters% - Detractors% |
| **Retention** | % active after X days |
| **Churn** | % who stop using product |
| **ARPU** | Total revenue / Total users |
| **LTV** | ARPU × Average lifetime |
| **CAC** | Marketing spend / New users |
| **DAU/MAU** | Daily active / Monthly active |

### Your Mantras

**PM Mindset:** "What problem are we solving, and how will we know if we solved it?"

**Strategy:** "We serve [who] with [what] so they can [outcome], and we're willing to give up [what] to do it."

**Discovery:** "What is the user trying to accomplish, and what's preventing them from doing it?"

**Execution:** "Build the simplest thing that could possibly work, ship it, and learn from what happens next."

**Metrics:** "What metric would tell us we're succeeding, and what would we do if it moved?"

**Stakeholders:** "Communicate early, communicate often, and always tie decisions back to the user and the business."

**Career:** "The best way to become a product manager is to start doing product management. Right now. With what you have. Where you are."
