## SECTION 1.1: SABSA Framework Quiz
### 10 Questions | 15 Minutes

---

**1. What does SABSA stand for?**

a) Security Architecture for Business Systems Analysis
b) Sherwood Applied Business Security Architecture
c) Systematic Approach to Business Security Architecture
d) Security and Business Systems Architecture

**Answer: B**
*Explanation: SABSA stands for Sherwood Applied Business Security Architecture. It is a business-driven security architecture methodology.*

---

**2. How many layers does the SABSA framework have?**

a) 4
b) 5
c) 6
d) 7

**Answer: C**
*Explanation: SABSA has 6 layers: Contextual, Conceptual, Logical, Physical, Component, and Operational.*

---

**3. Which SABSA layer answers the question "Why?"**

a) Logical Layer
b) Physical Layer
c) Conceptual Layer
d) Contextual Layer

**Answer: D**
*Explanation: The Contextual Layer answers "Why?" and defines the business requirements and environment.*

---

**4. What is the primary purpose of the SABSA traceability matrix?**

a) To track project budgets
b) To connect business requirements to operational procedures
c) To list all security controls
d) To document vendor relationships

**Answer: B**
*Explanation: The traceability matrix connects each layer to the one above and below it, ensuring business requirements are traceable to operational procedures.*

---

**5. Which SABSA layer defines the security strategy and service framework?**

a) Contextual Layer
b) Conceptual Layer
c) Logical Layer
d) Physical Layer

**Answer: B**
*Explanation: The Conceptual Layer (Layer 2) defines the security strategy and service framework—answering "What?"*

---

**6. The Physical Layer in SABSA answers which question?**

a) Why?
b) What?
c) How?
d) Where?

**Answer: D**
*Explanation: The Physical Layer answers "Where?" and defines the technology implementation.*

---

**7. Which SABSA layer is concerned with governance and operational procedures?**

a) Logical Layer
b) Physical Layer
c) Component Layer
d) Operational Layer

**Answer: D**
*Explanation: The Operational Layer defines governance, procedures, and how security is operated.*

---

**8. What is a key benefit of using SABSA?**

a) It focuses only on technology
b) It ensures security controls are aligned with business objectives
c) It eliminates the need for risk assessment
d) It only works for cloud environments

**Answer: B**
*Explanation: SABSA's primary benefit is business alignment—ensuring every security control supports a business objective.*

---

**9. The Component Layer in SABSA answers which question?**

a) Why?
b) What?
c) How?
d) Who/Which?

**Answer: D**
*Explanation: The Component Layer answers "Who/Which?" and defines detailed component specifications.*

---

**10. Which of the following is NOT one of the six SABSA layers?**

a) Contextual
b) Conceptual
c) Functional
d) Operational

**Answer: C**
*Explanation: The six SABSA layers are Contextual, Conceptual, Logical, Physical, Component, and Operational. "Functional" is not a SABSA layer.*

b) Spoofing

a) 5
b) 6
c) 7
d) 8

**Answer: C**
*Explanation: PASTA has 7 stages: Define Business Objectives, Define Technical Scope, Application Decomposition, Threat Analysis, Vulnerability Analysis, Attack Modeling, and Risk & Impact Analysis.*

---

**4. Which threat modeling methodology is most business-aligned?**

a) STRIDE
b) PASTA
c) Attack Trees
d) MITRE ATT&CK

**Answer: B**
*Explanation: PASTA is designed to be business-aligned, starting with business objectives and working backward to threats.*

---

**5. In an Attack Tree, what does the root node represent?**

a) A specific vulnerability
b) The attacker's goal
c) A security control
d) A mitigation strategy

**Answer: B**
*Explanation: The root node of an Attack Tree represents the attacker's ultimate goal.*

---

**6. What is the primary purpose of Attack Trees?**

a) To list all security controls
b) To visually represent attack paths
c) To document vulnerabilities
d) To define security policies

**Answer: B**
*Explanation: Attack Trees visually represent attack paths from the attacker's goal to individual attack steps.*

---

**7. MITRE ATT&CK provides a knowledge base of:**

a) Security controls
b) Adversary tactics and techniques
c) Compliance requirements
d) Technology standards

**Answer: B**
*Explanation: MITRE ATT&CK is a knowledge base of adversary tactics, techniques, and procedures.*

---

**8. How many tactics are in MITRE ATT&CK?**

a) 10
b) 12
c) 14
d) 16

**Answer: C**
*Explanation: MITRE ATT&CK has 14 tactics, from Reconnaissance to Impact.*

**9. Which MITRE ATT&CK technique is associated with credential dumping?**

d) T1068

**10. What is the purpose of mapping controls to MITRE ATT&CK techniques?**
