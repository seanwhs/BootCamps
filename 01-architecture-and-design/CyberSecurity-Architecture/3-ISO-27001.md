## The Target: ISO/IEC 27001 ISMS for Nexus Global Industries

In this section, we'll build the complete **ISO/IEC 27001 Information Security Management System (ISMS)** for Nexus Global Industries. ISO 27001 is the international standard for information security management, providing a systematic approach to managing sensitive company information.

**What specific file(s) are we building?**

- `iso27001/02_risk_assessment.md` - Risk assessment methodology and results
- `iso27001/03_statement_of_applicability.xlsx` - SoA with Annex A controls

- `iso27001/05_annex_a_control_mapping.csv` - Detailed Annex A control implementation
- `iso27001/06_isms_metrics.md` - ISMS performance metrics and KPIs

## The Concept: ISO 27001 in Plain English

Think of ISO 27001 like **building a quality management system for security**:

**ISMS Policy:** "We have a written commitment to security that everyone follows." This is your security mission statement and scope.

**Risk Assessment:** "We identify all the ways our information could be harmed and how likely each is." This is understanding your threats and vulnerabilities.

**Risk Treatment:** "For each risk, we decide what to do: fix it, accept it, avoid it, or transfer it." This is creating your action plan.

**Statement of Applicability (SoA):** "We have a checklist of 114 security controls. We've decided which ones apply to us and why." This is your control selection framework.

**Annex A Controls:** "Here are the specific security measures we're implementing (access control, encryption, etc.)." This is your detailed control implementation.

**Continuous Improvement:** "We regularly review and improve our security to keep up with threats." This is the Plan-Do-Check-Act cycle.

ISO 27001 is about **systematic, documented, and continuously improving security management**—not just a checklist of controls.

# ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Information Security Management System (ISMS)
## Nexus Global Industries

## Policy Statement

Nexus Global Industries is committed to protecting the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of all information assets under our control. This commitment is essential to maintaining the trust of our customers, partners, and employees, and to ensuring the continuity and success of our business operations.

We have established an Information Security Management System (ISMS) in accordance with ISO/IEC 27001:2022 to systematically manage information security risks, comply with regulatory requirements, and continuously improve our security posture.

## ISMS Scope

### Organizational Scope

The ISMS applies to all Nexus Global Industries entities, including:

1. **Nexus Global Industries HQ (Singapore)**
   - All corporate functions (Executive, Finance, HR, Legal)
   - Global security operations center (SOC)
   - Cloud infrastructure management

2. **Regional Hubs**
   - London (EMEA operations)
   - Austin (Americas operations)
   - Tokyo (APAC operations)

3. **Manufacturing Facilities (12 locations globally)**
   - OT/ICS environments (SCADA, PLC, DCS)
   - Quality control systems
   - IoT sensor networks

4. **R&D Centers**
   - Austin (AI/ML research)
   - Singapore (Manufacturing innovation)
   - London (Industry 4.0)

5. **Cloud Environments**
   - AWS (primary, 60% workloads)
   - Azure (secondary, 20% workloads)

6. **Kubernetes Clusters (15 production clusters)**
   - All containerized workloads (200+ microservices)

### Scope Boundaries

The ISMS scope includes all systems, applications, data, and personnel that support the following business processes:

| Process | Description | Criticality |
|---------|-------------|-------------|
| **Manufacturing Operations** | PLC/SCADA control, production planning, quality control | CRITICAL |
| **Customer Portal** | B2B ordering, specifications, support | CRITICAL |
| **R&D and IP Management** | AI/ML research, manufacturing recipes, digital twins | CRITICAL |
| **Financial Operations** | ERP, payments, procurement, payroll | HIGH |
| **HR and Employee Management** | Employee data, benefits, performance | HIGH |
| **Internal Collaboration** | Email, messaging, document sharing | MEDIUM |
| **External Communication** | Marketing, public website, customer support | LOW |

### Scope Exclusions

The following are **excluded** from the current ISMS scope:

- Legacy systems not scheduled for modernization (under exception management)
- Third-party SaaS platforms (managed via vendor risk management)
- Non-production development environments (excluded, but must follow security guidelines)

### ISMS Objectives

1. **Confidentiality**: Ensure information is accessible only to authorized personnel.
2. **Integrity**: Safeguard the accuracy and completeness of information.
3. **Availability**: Ensure information is accessible when needed by authorized users.
4. **Compliance**: Meet all applicable legal, regulatory, and contractual requirements.
5. **Business Continuity**: Ensure business operations can continue during and after incidents.
6. **Continuous Improvement**: Systematically improve security through monitoring and review.

### Regulatory and Legal Requirements

The ISMS addresses all applicable regulations:

1. **GDPR** (EU General Data Protection Regulation)
2. **NIST SP 800-171** (US Government contracts)
3. **CCPA/CPRA** (California Consumer Privacy Act)
4. **PDPA** (Singapore Personal Data Protection Act)
5. **Local Manufacturing Regulations** (12 countries)
6. **Export Controls** (US and international)
7. **Contractual Requirements** (customer SLAs, vendor agreements)

### Management Commitment

The Executive Leadership Team (ELT) and Board of Directors are committed to:

- Allocating adequate resources for ISMS implementation and maintenance
- Supporting risk-based decision-making
- Ensuring security is integrated into business processes
- Providing regular oversight and review
- Fostering a culture of security awareness and responsibility

# ISO/IEC 27001 Risk Assessment - Nexus Global Industries

## Risk Assessment Methodology

### Approach

Nexus Global Industries adopts a **quantitative-qualitative hybrid** risk assessment methodology aligned with ISO 31000:2018 and ISO/IEC 27005:2022.

### Risk Identification

Risks are identified through:

1. **Asset Inventory**: All assets (information, software, hardware, services, people) are cataloged
2. **Threat Modeling**: STRIDE, PASTA, and Attack Trees are used
3. **Vulnerability Scanning**: Continuous scanning (Qualys, AWS Inspector)
4. **Incident History**: Previous security incidents and near-misses
5. **Threat Intelligence**: MITRE ATT&CK, vendor threat intelligence
6. **Stakeholder Input**: Business owners, IT teams, security teams

### Risk Analysis

**Likelihood Criteria:**

| Score | Likelihood | Description | Frequency |
|-------|------------|-------------|-----------|
| 1 | Very Low | Highly unlikely, no known occurrences | < 1 in 10 years |
| 2 | Low | Unlikely, rare occurrences | 1 in 5-10 years |
| 3 | Moderate | Possible, occasional occurrences | 1 in 2-5 years |
| 4 | High | Likely, frequent occurrences | 1 in 1-2 years |
| 5 | Very High | Almost certain, continuous | ≥ 1 per year |

**Impact Criteria:**

| Score | Impact | Description | Business Consequence |
|-------|--------|-------------|---------------------|
| 1 | Low | Minimal impact, easily recoverable | < $100K loss |
| 2 | Medium | Moderate impact, some recovery | $100K - $500K loss |
| 3 | High | Significant impact, major recovery effort | $500K - $2M loss |
| 4 | Very High | Severe impact, significant recovery | $2M - $5M loss |
| 5 | Critical | Catastrophic impact, business threatening | > $5M loss |

**Risk Score = Likelihood × Impact**

**Risk Level Matrix:**

| | Impact 1 | Impact 2 | Impact 3 | Impact 4 | Impact 5 |
|---|----------|----------|----------|----------|----------|
| **Likelihood 1** | 1 (LOW) | 2 (LOW) | 3 (LOW) | 4 (MEDIUM) | 5 (MEDIUM) |
| **Likelihood 2** | 2 (LOW) | 4 (MEDIUM) | 6 (MEDIUM) | 8 (HIGH) | 10 (HIGH) |
| **Likelihood 3** | 3 (LOW) | 6 (MEDIUM) | 9 (HIGH) | 12 (HIGH) | 15 (CRITICAL) |
| **Likelihood 4** | 4 (MEDIUM) | 8 (HIGH) | 12 (HIGH) | 16 (CRITICAL) | 20 (CRITICAL) |
| **Likelihood 5** | 5 (MEDIUM) | 10 (HIGH) | 15 (CRITICAL) | 20 (CRITICAL) | 25 (CRITICAL) |

**Risk Levels:**

- **LOW (1-4)**: Accept or monitor
- **MEDIUM (5-9)**: Plan treatment within 6 months
- **HIGH (10-14)**: Treat within 3 months
- **CRITICAL (15-25)**: Treat immediately (within 30 days)

## Risk Treatment Strategy

### Treatment Options

For each identified risk, one of the following treatment options is selected:

1. **Mitigate**: Implement controls to reduce risk to acceptable level
2. **Accept**: Accept the risk (with justification and approval)
3. **Avoid**: Stop the activity causing the risk
4. **Transfer**: Transfer the risk to a third party (insurance, outsourcing)

### Treatment Prioritization

| Risk Level | Treatment Timeline |
|------------|-------------------|
| CRITICAL (15-25) | Within 30 days |
| HIGH (10-14) | Within 3 months |
