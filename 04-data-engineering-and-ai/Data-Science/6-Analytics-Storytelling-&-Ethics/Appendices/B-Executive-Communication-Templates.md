# Appendix B: Executive Communication Templates

## Overview

This appendix provides ready-to-use templates for all your executive communication needs. Think of these as your "communication toolkit" - whenever you need to present data to leadership, you can grab the appropriate template and customize it.

### What's Included
1. **Executive Summary Template** - For strategic recommendations
2. **Dashboard Commentary Template** - For explaining visualizations
3. **Email Templates** - For different stakeholder types
4. **Meeting Agenda Templates** - For leadership reviews
5. **Action Item Templates** - For tracking decisions
6. **Q&A Preparation Templates** - For anticipating questions
7. **Follow-up Communication Templates** - For maintaining momentum

## Response Strategies

### For Tough Questions
1. **Acknowledge** - "That's an excellent question..."
2. **Clarify** - "Let me make sure I understand your question..."
3. **Respond** - Provide clear, concise answer
4. **Bridge** - Connect back to key message
5. **Close** - "Does that address your concern?"

### For Questions You Can't Answer
1. **Acknowledge** - "That's a great question"
2. **Commit** - "I don't have that information right now"
3. **Promise** - "I will get back to you with the answer"
4. **Follow-up** - "I'll ensure you have the information by [date]"

---

1. **[Message 1]** - [Why it matters]
2. **[Message 2]** - [Why it matters]
3. **[Message 3]** - [Why it matters]

- "What concerns do you have about this approach?"
- "What additional information would be helpful?"

---

2. **Address outstanding questions:** Within 48 hours
3. **Schedule follow-up:** If needed
4. **Document decisions:** Immediately

*This document is for presenter preparation only.*
