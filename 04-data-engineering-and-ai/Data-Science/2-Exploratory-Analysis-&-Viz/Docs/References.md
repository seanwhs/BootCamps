# SECTION 10: GLOSSARY OF TERMS

## A

**ANOVA (Analysis of Variance):** Statistical method for comparing means of three or more groups.

**Automated EDA Tools:** Software that generates comprehensive data exploration reports automatically.

## B

**Bias-Variance Tradeoff:** The balance between model complexity and generalization.

**Bivariate Analysis:** Analysis of relationships between two variables.

**Box Plot:** Visualization showing five-number summary (min, Q1, median, Q3, max).

**Broadcasting:** NumPy's ability to perform operations on arrays of different shapes.

## C

**Cardinality:** The number of unique values in a categorical variable.

**Central Limit Theorem:** The distribution of sample means approaches normal as sample size increases.

**Central Tendency:** Measures representing the center of a distribution (mean, median, mode).

**Confidence Interval:** Range of values likely containing the true population parameter.

**Correlation:** Measure of the strength and direction of a relationship between variables.

**Cramér's V:** Measure of association between categorical variables.

**Cross-Filtering:** Selecting data in one chart to filter data in another chart.

## D

**Data-Ink Ratio:** Proportion of ink used to display data vs. total ink in a visualization.

**Data Leakage:** When information from outside the training dataset is used in modeling.

**Declarative Programming:** Describing what you want, not how to build it.

**Dispersion:** Measures of spread in a distribution (std, variance, IQR, range).

## E

**EDA (Exploratory Data Analysis):** Process of understanding data before formal modeling.

**Encoding:** Mapping data to visual properties (x, y, color, size).

## F

**Faceting:** Creating multiple charts based on categorical variables.

**Feature Engineering:** Creating new features from existing data.

## G

**Gestalt Principles:** Laws of perception applied to visualization design.

**Grammar of Graphics:** Framework for describing visualizations.

**GridSpec:** Matplotlib component for controlling subplot layout.

## H

**Heteroscedasticity:** Non-constant variance in data.

**Homoscedasticity:** Constant variance in data.

## I

**Imputation:** Filling missing values with estimated values.

**Interquartile Range (IQR):** Difference between Q3 and Q1.

## K

**Kurtosis:** Measure of "tailedness" of a distribution.

## L

**Leptokurtic:** Distribution with heavy tails and high peak.

## M

**MCAR (Missing Completely at Random):** Missingness independent of all data.

**MAR (Missing at Random):** Missingness depends on observed data.

**Mesokurtic:** Distribution with normal kurtosis.

**MNAR (Missing Not at Random):** Missingness depends on missing values.

**Multicollinearity:** High correlation between predictor variables.

**Multivariate Analysis:** Analysis of relationships among three or more variables.

## N

**Normal Distribution:** Symmetric bell-shaped distribution.

## O

**Outlier:** Data point significantly different from others.

## P

**p-value:** Probability of observing data if null hypothesis is true.

**Pair Plot:** Matrix showing pairwise relationships between variables.

**Pearson Correlation:** Measure of linear relationship between variables.

**Platykurtic:** Distribution with light tails and flat peak.

## R

**R² Score:** Proportion of variance explained by a model.

**Regression:** Predicting continuous values.

## S

**Skewness:** Measure of distribution asymmetry.

**Spearman Correlation:** Measure of monotonic relationship based on ranks.

**Synthetic Data:** Artificially generated data mimicking real patterns.

## U

**Univariate Analysis:** Analysis of individual variables.

**Upsampling:** Increasing the frequency of time series data.

## V

**Variance:** Average squared deviation from the mean.

**Vectorization:** Performing operations on entire arrays rather than looping.

**VIF (Variance Inflation Factor):** Measure of multicollinearity.

**Violin Plot:** Distribution plot showing KDE and summary statistics.

## 11.1 Statistical Concepts Reference

| Concept | Formula | When to Use |
|---------|---------|-------------|
| Mean | Σx / n | Symmetric distributions |
| Median | Middle value | Skewed distributions |
| Mode | Most frequent | Categorical data |
| Variance | Σ(x-μ)²/n | Mathematical convenience |
| Std Dev | √Variance | Original units |
| IQR | Q3-Q1 | Robust spread measure |
| Pearson r | Σ((x-μx)(y-μy))/(nσxσy) | Linear relationships |
| Spearman ρ | 1-6Σd²/(n(n²-1)) | Monotonic relationships |
| Cramér's V | √(χ²/(n·min(k-1,r-1))) | Categorical association |

```python
df = pd.read_csv('data.csv')

df.head()
df.info()

plt.show()

# Basic scatter
plt.scatter(df['x'], df['y'])
plt.xlabel('X')
plt.ylabel('Y')
plt.show()
```
