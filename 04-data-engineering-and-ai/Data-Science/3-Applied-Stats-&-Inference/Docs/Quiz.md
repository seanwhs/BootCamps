### Short Answer (91-100)

**91.** Standard deviation measures the spread of data around the mean. Standard error measures the precision of the sample mean as an estimate of the population mean. SE = SD/√n.

**92.** When you have a specific directional hypothesis and only care about whether the effect is in that direction (e.g., "treatment increases conversion").

**93.** L: Linearity (relationship is linear), I: Independence (observations are independent), N: Normality (residuals are normally distributed), E: Equal variance (homoscedasticity).

**94.** FWER (Family-Wise Error Rate) controls the probability of making at least one false positive across multiple tests. FDR (False Discovery Rate) controls the expected proportion of false positives among rejected hypotheses. FWER is more conservative.

**95.** When data is not normal, when working with ordinal data, when sample size is small, or when outliers are present. Non-parametric tests are more robust and make fewer assumptions.

**96.** VIF > 10 indicates severe multicollinearity, meaning the predictor variable is highly correlated with other predictors. This suggests removing the variable or using regularization.

**97.** To verify that model assumptions are met, ensuring the model is valid and the results are reliable. Diagnostics check for linearity, normality, independence, homoscedasticity, and influential points.

**98.** Linear regression is used for continuous outcomes. Logistic regression is used for binary outcomes (0/1). The choice depends on the nature of the dependent variable.

**99.** The CLT states that the distribution of sample means becomes approximately normal as sample size increases, regardless of the population distribution (provided it has finite variance). This makes inference possible even with non-normal data.

**100.** Statistical significance means the result is unlikely due to chance (p < α). Practical significance means the effect is large enough to be meaningful in the real world. A result can be statistically significant but practically meaningless.
